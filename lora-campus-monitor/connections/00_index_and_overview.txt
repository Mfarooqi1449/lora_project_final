The three units are NOT wired to each other. Each one has its own
power supply and its own antenna. The only link between them is LoRa.

 Ra-02 MISO   GPIO19                Pin 21 / GPIO9
 Ra-02 MOSI   GPIO23                Pin 19 / GPIO10
 Ra-02 NSS    GPIO5                 Pin 24 / GPIO8 (CE0)

 I2C SDA      GPIO21                -
 I2C SCL      GPIO22                -
 MQ-135 AO    GPIO34 (via divider)  -
 Sound AO     GPIO35                -
 Supply mon.  GPIO32 (optional)     -

GOLDEN RULES
------------
 1. Ra-02 = 3.3 V only.  2. ESP32 inputs = 3.3 V max.
 3. Antenna on before power.  4. Common GND inside each unit.
 5. Same radio settings on all three LoRa modules.
